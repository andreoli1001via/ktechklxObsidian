# CACEIS Bank obtains MiCA authorisation

📅 06/30/2025

CACEIS Bank was authorised by the **ACPR** (Autorité de Contrôle Prudentiel et de Résolution — France's banking and insurance regulator), following the advice of the **AMF** (Autorité des Marchés Financiers — France's financial sector regulator), to provide crypto-asset services in accordance with the **Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation (MiCA)**.

## What is MiCA?

The Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation (MiCA) is the EU regulatory framework governing crypto-asset service providers. It establishes authorisation requirements, operational standards, and consumer protection rules for firms offering crypto-asset services in the European Union.

MiCA came into force in December 2024, replacing the previous French PSAN (Prestataires de Services sur Actifs Numériques) registration regime.

## CACEIS's Authorised Services Under MiCA

With its MiCA licence, CACEIS Bank is authorised to provide:
- **Custody and administration of crypto-assets** on behalf of clients
- **Execution of orders** for crypto-assets on behalf of clients (RTO — Réception et Transmission d'Ordres)

This makes CACEIS one of the first traditional asset servicers to hold a MiCA licence, enabling it to offer **institutional-grade crypto-asset custody and execution** to its existing client base.

## Strategic Significance

The MiCA authorisation aligns with CACEIS's ACT 2028 medium-term plan, which targets:
- **Quadrupling digital assets under custody within three years**
- Developing digital asset tokenisation services
- Becoming a key infrastructure provider for the tokenised fund market

CACEIS already acts as:
- Transfer agent and tokenisation agent for Amundi tokenised money market funds
- Depositary for the world's first natively tokenised IPO on Lise exchange (April 2026)
- Transfer and tokenisation agent in the Amundi-Ant International blockchain treasury collaboration
