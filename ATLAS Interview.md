# Atlas by Caceis – Our integrated, front-to-back asset servicing model

📅 05/06/2026

Building on our middle office expertise, CACEIS is enhancing its front-to-back asset servicing model with **ATLAS** — combining support across the entire investment lifecycle with in-depth operational expertise and ongoing technological innovation to offer investment managers greater value and oversight.

## What is ATLAS?

ATLAS is CACEIS' response to the investment management community's evolving needs. In the current market environment — characterised by growing regulatory requirements, shorter settlement cycles (T+1), and increasing operational complexity — many investment managers are reassessing the efficiency of their operating models.

ATLAS provides:
- **Front-to-Back integration**: A single, seamless operational chain from order generation through settlement, custody and accounting
- **Middle Office outsourcing**: CACEIS assumes responsibility for trade lifecycle management, reconciliation, and exception handling
- **Greater oversight**: Investment managers gain full visibility across portfolios and operations via the OLIS portal
- **Scalability**: Infrastructure built to accommodate growth in assets, strategies, and complexity without proportional cost increases

## Key Capabilities

| Layer | CACEIS Service |
|---|---|
| Front Office Support | Order Management, Execution connectivity, Pre-trade compliance |
| Middle Office | Trade capture, Reconciliation, Corporate actions, Collateral management |
| Back Office | Settlement, Custody, Fund Accounting, Performance attribution |
| Reporting | OLIS portal, ESG/Climate reporting, Regulatory reporting |

## Why ATLAS Now?

Several market pressures are driving demand for integrated front-to-back models:
- **T+1 settlement**: Accelerated settlement cycles (US already T+1, EU moves toward it) require faster, more automated processing chains
- **Regulatory complexity**: EMIR, AIFMD, CSDR, DORA and others add layers of reporting and compliance requirements
- **Cost pressure**: Asset managers seek to reduce operational costs through outsourcing while maintaining quality
- **Data demands**: Institutional clients increasingly require real-time, granular data on their portfolios

## CACEIS Differentiators

CACEIS operates ATLAS from a position of unique strength: as a top-5 European custodian, we already have access to clients' full portfolio data, enabling deeper integration and fewer interfaces. The ATLAS model reduces the number of counterparties, minimises reconciliation breaks, and provides a single point of accountability across the investment lifecycle.
