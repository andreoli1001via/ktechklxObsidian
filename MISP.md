# MISP: strengthening financial market integration in Europe

📅 04/22/2026

The European Commission's proposed **Market Integration and Supervision Package (MISP)** is designed to remove key barriers to capital market integration in Europe. As a major European asset servicer operating across multiple jurisdictions, CACEIS has significant expertise in the cross-border challenges that MISP aims to address.

## What is MISP?

The Market Integration and Supervision Package is a European Commission initiative aimed at:
- Removing fragmentation in European capital markets
- Streamlining cross-border securities settlement
- Harmonising supervisory standards across EU member states
- Reducing compliance costs for market participants operating across borders

MISP builds on earlier initiatives including the Capital Markets Union (CMU) action plan and is part of the broader European Savings and Investment Union (SIU) strategy.

## Key Provisions

### Post-Trade Harmonisation
A central component of MISP is the simplification of post-trade infrastructure across the EU. Key measures include:
- Reducing T+2 settlement failures penalties and aligning with T+1 transition preparations
- Harmonising CSDR rules across member states
- Creating a more unified European collateral framework

### Cross-Border Distribution
MISP aims to simplify the cross-border distribution of investment funds by:
- Reducing administrative barriers to passporting
- Harmonising KYC/AML requirements across member states
- Streamlining investor registration and notification processes

### Supervisory Convergence
- Greater ESMA (European Securities and Markets Authority) coordination
- Harmonised supervisory expectations across NCAs (National Competent Authorities)
- Reduced duplication of regulatory filings
