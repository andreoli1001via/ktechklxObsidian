# CACEIS reaffirms its role as a strategic partner for investors committed to sustainable investment

📅 06/25/2026

Since **January 2025**, CACEIS has been supporting the development of sustainable investment strategies by **waiving its fees for one year** on all new fund launches or sub-funds classified as **Article 8 or Article 9** under the EU Sustainable Finance Disclosure Regulation (SFDR).

## What Are Article 8 and Article 9 Funds?

Under the EU SFDR (Sustainable Finance Disclosure Regulation):
- **Article 8 funds**: Promote environmental and/or social characteristics
- **Article 9 funds**: Have sustainable investment as their objective (the most stringent ESG classification)

By waiving fees on new Article 8 and 9 fund launches, CACEIS is providing a direct financial incentive for asset managers to grow their sustainable fund range.

## CACEIS ESG Strategy

CACEIS supports clients' sustainable investment obligations across multiple dimensions:

### Regulatory Support
- SFDR reporting and classification support
- EU Taxonomy alignment assessment
- CSRD (Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive) data integration
- ESG portfolio disclosure for UCITS and AIFs

### ESG Data and Analytics
- Carbon assessment and climate risk reporting
- ESG portfolio monitoring via OLIS portal
- Sustainability indicators aligned with TCFD (Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures)
- Scope 1, 2, and 3 emission tracking

### PERES (Private Equity Real Estate Solutions)
- Directing equity bridge financing specifically to Article 8 and 9 funds
- Trained operational team focused on sustainable finance
- ESG due diligence support for private market assets

### Custodian Support
- Proxy voting management aligned with ESG principles
- ESG data integration in fund accounting
- Climate risk assessment reports
