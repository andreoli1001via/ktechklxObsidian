# CACEIS and Triodos Investment Management strengthen partnership with new servicing mandate for Dutch Article 9 funds

📅 04/21/2026

**Triodos Investment Management** has selected CACEIS, a leading European asset servicing group, for its **Dutch Article 9 fund range**, building on the existing relationship across other fund ranges.

## About Triodos Investment Management

Triodos Investment Management is a global sustainable and impact investor, wholly owned by Triodos Bank. It manages funds focused exclusively on sustainable and impact investments across:
- Sustainable food and agriculture
- Renewable energy and climate
- Inclusive finance
- Arts and culture
- Sustainable real estate

All Triodos Investment Management funds are classified as Article 9 under SFDR — the most rigorous sustainable investment classification in the EU.

## Services CACEIS Will Provide

For the Dutch Article 9 fund range, CACEIS will provide:
- **Depositary services**: Oversight and safekeeping function required under AIFMD and UCITS
- **Fund administration**: NAV calculation, fund accounting, regulatory reporting
- **Transfer agent services**: Investor register, subscription and redemption processing

## Strategic Significance

This mandate demonstrates CACEIS's capability in servicing **Article 9 (darkest green) funds**, which have the strictest ESG reporting and investment constraints. It also reflects CACEIS's fee waiver initiative for new Article 8 and 9 fund launches (in place since January 2025).

The Netherlands is a key growth market for CACEIS, which provides:
- Transfer agent services from Amsterdam
- Pension fund front-to-back solutions (key market given large Dutch pension sector)
- Sub-custody services in the Dutch market
- Reporting tailored to Dutch regulatory requirements (AFM, DNB)
