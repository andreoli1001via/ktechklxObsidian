# CACEIS - European Expertise with North American Proximity

📅 04/20/2026

A look inside our growing US presence — helping asset managers navigate the changing financial landscape with a full commitment to the Crédit Agricole group's core values.

## CACEIS in North America

CACEIS has established a growing presence in North America, serving European asset managers with US operations and US asset managers seeking European market access. The North American offering combines CACEIS's deep European expertise with on-the-ground US proximity.

## Key Services for North American Clients

### Global Custody & Sub-Custody
CACEIS provides global custody services for US managers investing in European markets, and serves as European sub-custodian for US global custodians. Deep expertise in:
- European equity markets (Frankfurt, Paris, London, Amsterdam, Milan, Madrid)
- European fixed income markets
- European alternatives and private markets

### Fund Administration for Cross-Border Structures
Many US asset managers use European fund structures (UCITS, AIFs) to access European retail distribution:
- CACEIS provides fund administration for US managers' European fund ranges
- NAV calculation, risk reporting, and regulatory filings for UCITS and AIF structures
- Transfer agent services in Luxembourg (a key hub for US managers' European UCITS)

### Market Solutions for USD/EUR Flows
- FX spot and forward execution (USD/EUR and other pairs)
- Currency Overlay services for US managers with EUR-denominated portfolios
- Derivatives clearing (US managers accessing European clearing venues)

## Why North American Proximity Matters

CACEIS's ACT 2028 medium-term plan includes opening a branch in **Singapore** to support European clientele and build Asian business. The North American strategy follows a similar logic: being physically present near clients while delivering European expertise.

The Crédit Agricole Group's broader presence in the US (via Crédit Agricole CIB's US operations) supports CACEIS's North American strategy with established relationships and infrastructure.

## WebReporting Platform
CACEIS provides a dedicated **WebReporting – North America** platform (`caceis-investorservices.olisnet.com`) for North American clients.
