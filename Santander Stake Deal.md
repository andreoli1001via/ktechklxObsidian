# Crédit Agricole S.A. completes the acquisition of Santander's 30.5% stake in CACEIS — now 100% owner

📅 07/04/2025

Following the agreement announced on December 19th, 2024, and after receiving all required regulatory authorizations, **Crédit Agricole S.A. announces having completed the acquisition of Santander's 30.5% stake in CACEIS**.

## Key Facts

- **Crédit Agricole S.A. now owns 100% of CACEIS**
- Previous ownership structure: Crédit Agricole 69.5% + Santander 30.5%
- Agreement to acquire Santander's stake announced: December 19, 2024
- Completion: July 4, 2025 (following receipt of all required authorizations)

## Background

CACEIS was created in 2005 through the merger of Crédit Lyonnais Investor Services and Fastnet (a subsidiary of Crédit Agricole). Santander became a shareholder when it contributed its European asset servicing activities to CACEIS.

In July 2023, CACEIS completed the acquisition of **Royal Bank of Canada's European asset servicing activities**, which significantly strengthened its European position.

The acquisition of Santander's stake marks the completion of CACEIS's transition to a **fully Crédit Agricole-owned entity**, enabling closer strategic alignment with the Crédit Agricole Group's ACT 2028 medium-term plan.

## Strategic Implications

Full ownership by Crédit Agricole S.A. allows:
- Deeper integration with Crédit Agricole Group's "Premium Client Solutions" offering
- Stronger alignment with ACT 2028 strategic priorities (digital assets, ETFs, PERES expansion)
- More streamlined capital allocation decisions
- Enhanced cross-selling with Crédit Agricole CIB and Indosuez Wealth Management

## Governance Change
Following this transaction, **Jean-François Balaÿ** (CEO of Crédit Agricole CIB) was appointed Chairman of the CACEIS Board of Directors on May 15, 2025.
