# Regulatory watch

The regulatory watch section, published by CACEIS's experts, provides essential information on the main National, European and International regulatory developments that impact our clients' activities and the sphere of Asset Servicing.

For each regulation: main provisions, key dates, scope of application, and related articles.

**RegWatch** — CACEIS's monthly regulatory monitoring publication — provides a focused overview of regulatory developments in countries where CACEIS is present. Customers wishing to receive RegWatch should contact their usual CACEIS contact.

## Regulatory Framework Coverage

### AEOI — Automatic Exchange of Information
Framework for international automatic exchange of financial account information for tax purposes.

### AIFM — Alternative Investment Fund Managers Directive
European directive governing the management and marketing of alternative investment funds, including private equity, real estate and hedge funds.

### BREXIT
Regulatory impact and adaptations resulting from the United Kingdom's departure from the European Union.

### CSDR — Central Securities Depositories Regulation
Regulation governing settlement discipline and the authorisation of central securities depositories in Europe.

### ELTIF — European Long-Term Investment Fund
Regulation enabling the creation of long-term investment funds accessible to retail and institutional investors. ELTIF 2.0 authorises distribution to retail clients, permits redemptions during the fund's life, and eases liquidity and diversification rules.

### EMIR — European Market Infrastructure Regulation
Regulation requiring central clearing of OTC derivatives and reporting of all derivative contracts to trade repositories.

### FATCA — Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act
US legislation requiring foreign financial institutions to report on US persons' accounts to the IRS.

### MiFID / MiFIR — Markets in Financial Instruments Directive/Regulation
European framework governing financial markets, investment services, trading venues, and investor protection.

### MMF — Money Market Fund Regulation
Regulation governing money market funds, including requirements on portfolio composition, liquidity and stress testing.

### PRIIPS — Packaged Retail and Insurance-based Investment Products
Regulation requiring manufacturers to produce Key Information Documents (KIDs) for retail investors.

### PSAN — Prestataires de Services sur Actifs Numériques
French regulatory framework for digital asset service providers. CACEIS holds a MiCA licence for institutional crypto-asset execution and custody.

### Solvency II
European regulatory framework for insurance companies, establishing capital requirements and risk management standards.

### UCITS V — Undertakings for Collective Investment in Transferable Securities
European framework governing collective investment schemes sold to retail investors, establishing requirements for depositaries, remuneration policies and sanctions.
