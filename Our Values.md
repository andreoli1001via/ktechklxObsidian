# Our values

## Culture "We are ONE CACEIS"

In recent years, CACEIS has grown significantly through successive mergers and integrations to become a Group of more than **7,000 employees** with an increasingly international footprint in **17 countries**. Facing a strong integration/cultural challenge, CACEIS has defined a common culture aiming at leveraging our differences, creating a sense of belonging and fostering motivation to better serve its clients.

## Our Four Values

CACEIS has defined a set of corporate values that employees can relate to and that guide actions on a daily basis. These values are the cornerstone of its common culture and the mark of belonging to the Group. They apply both internally and with clients.

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **We care** | We care of our clients and colleagues |
| **We dare** | We promote personal empowerment and encourage initiatives |
| **We grow** | We grow alongside our clients, both as a company and as individuals whilst understanding that everyone is different |
| **We are ONE CACEIS** | Team spirit, unity and solidarity |

Through these four values, CACEIS anchors the common culture "We are ONE CACEIS" in its identity, a culture that inspires and empowers, animating behaviours and working methods in all Group entities. These values are fully in line with the Crédit Agricole group's Human-Centric Project.
