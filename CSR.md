# Corporate Social Responsibility

## EcoVadis Gold Rating — Top 2% in Financial Services

CACEIS has integrated **Corporate Social Responsibility and ESG principles throughout its value chain**, in all its business sectors and at all levels.

In 2024, CACEIS was awarded a **Gold medal in the EcoVadis 2024 audit** and is now in the **top 2% of companies assessed by EcoVadis** in the Financial Services sector (excluding insurance and pension funds).

Score: **80/100** — up 7 points compared to 2023.

The score is based on analysis across categories:
- **Environment**: greenhouse gas emissions, energy consumption, water, paper, waste, and impact of financial products on sustainable consumption
- **Labor and Human rights**: health and safety, working conditions, social dialogue, career management, training, discrimination and fundamental human rights
- **Ethics**: corruption, anti-competitive practices, responsible marketing
- **Sustainable Procurement**: environmental and social performance of providers

## Three CSR Pillars
1. Supporting clients with confidence and security
2. Taking action to promote the energy and environmental transition
3. Being a responsible and committed employer

## The Grameen Foundation
CACEIS in Luxembourg has accompanied the Grameen Crédit Agricole Microfinance Foundation since its launch by providing banking, administration, depository and custodial services. The Foundation was created in Luxembourg in September 2008 as a partnership between the Crédit Agricole Group and Prof. Muhammad Yunus (founder of the Grameen Bank, 2006 Nobel Peace Prize winner). Mission: fight poverty in developing countries through Microfinance and Social Business.
