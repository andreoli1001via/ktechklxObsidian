# CACEIS wins LATAM and European 'Custodian of the Year'!

📅 11/14/2025

At the **Asset Servicing Times** award ceremony in London, CACEIS took home two prestigious awards:
- **European Custodian of the Year**
- **LATAM Custodian of the Year**

## About the Awards

The Asset Servicing Times awards recognise excellence in the global asset servicing industry. The awards are voted for by industry professionals and are considered among the most prestigious in the custody and asset servicing sector.

## CACEIS's European Custody Leadership

CACEIS's European Custodian of the Year award reflects:
- **€5.9 trillion in assets under custody** (as of December 31, 2025)
- Presence across **18 European and international countries**
- Recent acquisition of **Royal Bank of Canada's European asset servicing activities** (July 2023)
- Full integration of Santander's European asset servicing business (acquired in prior years)
- Strong investment in technology (OLIS portal, AI Factory, ATLAS model)

## CACEIS in Latin America

The LATAM Custodian of the Year award recognises CACEIS's strong position in Latin American markets. CACEIS has significant operations in:
- **Brazil**: Major local presence serving local and international investors
- **Other LatAm markets**: Sub-custody and representative services

The LATAM operations benefit from the legacy of Santander Securities Services (inherited through the Santander partnership) and CACEIS's broader international network.

## Industry Recognition Context

Previous awards and recognitions:
- Multiple years of "Best Custodian" awards from industry publications
- Recognition for innovation in digital assets and ETF services
- EcoVadis Gold rating for CSR/ESG (top 2% in Financial Services)
