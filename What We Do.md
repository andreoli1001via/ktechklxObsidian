# What We Do

CACEIS offers a comprehensive range of asset servicing products and services across all asset classes. Services are available in Belgium, France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Luxembourg, Netherlands, Spain, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom.

## Core Service Categories

### Execution & Clearing
- Agency execution across 80+ markets and 100+ counterparties
- Derivatives clearing with end-to-end processing across 60+ markets
- EMIR reporting and 100% STP
- FX spot, forwards, swaps and NDF execution across 70+ currencies

### Custody & Depositary
- Global custody services across all time zones
- Depositary banking for UCITS, AIFM and PERES funds
- Securities lending and borrowing
- Collateral management and optimisation

### Fund Administration & Accounting
- NAV calculation and fund accounting
- Middle-office outsourcing
- Performance and risk reporting
- ESG and Climate reporting

### Transfer Agent & Fund Distribution
- Transfer agent services in Luxembourg, Belgium, Ireland, Switzerland and Netherlands
- AML/KYC including FATCA and AEOI compliance
- Cross-border distribution of French funds
- Local representation in France, Luxembourg, Italy, Switzerland, Belgium, Germany, Netherlands and Ireland
- Digital account opening (KYC360) for retail and institutional investors
- TEEPI Market Place: subscription to 35,000 fund units, 68,000 ISIN codes
- Access to blockchain/DLT platforms

### Private Equity Real Estate Solutions (PERES)
Front-to-back integrated solutions for Private Equity, Real Estate, Infrastructure and Private Debt:
- Depositary Bank & Custody
- Fund Administration & Accounting
- Register Keeping & Transfer Agent
- Middle Office Outsourcing
- Domiciliary Agent & Company Secretary
- Fund Finance
- Market Solutions

Asset classes: Debt (public loans, private loans, hybrid funds), Real Estate (core plus, value add), Private Equity (LBO, Venture Capital, Growth Capital, Fund of Funds), Infrastructure (Hydrogen, Renewable Energy, Telecoms, Transportation, Healthcare).

### ETF Services
- ETF order management and settlement in Ireland (centre of excellence)
- Multiple settlement models: ICSD, ICSD+, and local CSDs
- Primary market order entry portal
- Fund accounting with early NAV delivery
- Portfolio Composition File (PCF) production via Ultumus partnership
- Support for Active ETFs and passive strategies

### Pension Fund Services
- Front to Back Solutions (Netherlands)
- Middle Office outsourcing for pension funds
- Full scope regulatory, compliance, risk, performance and ESG/Climate reporting

### Market Solutions
- Forex execution (spot, forwards, swaps, NDFs, 70+ currencies)
- Currency Overlay (fully outsourced hedging strategies)
- Agency Execution (multi-asset, 80+ markets, pure agency model, no mark-up)
- Derivatives Clearing (60+ markets, collateral optimisation)
- Securities Finance (lending, borrowing, financing, collateral optimisation)

### Issuer Services
- Proxy voting management
- Share registry management
- Corporate actions processing

## Key Technology Platforms
- OLIS: Secure web portal for real-time data and reporting
- KYC360: Digital onboarding for retail investors
- TEEPI: Institutional investor marketplace
- AI Factory: AI-powered data analytics and process automation
- ATLAS: Integrated front-to-back asset servicing model
