# The new liquidity landscape: Why integrated solutions are optimal for pension funds

📅 06/17/2026

*By Olivier Zemb (Head of Equity Finance and Collateral Management), Joanna Ksenzova (Senior Market Services Sales), and Rémy Ferraretto (Head of Securities Finance & Repo Sales) at CACEIS*

## The Evolving Liquidity Challenge for Pension Funds

Pension funds are increasingly being forced to rethink their approach to liquidity management. A combination of factors — rising interest rates, stricter regulatory requirements under AIFMD II, increased use of derivatives, and a structural shift in portfolio composition toward illiquid alternative assets — means that liquidity management is now a genuine strategic challenge.

## Why Integrated Solutions Are Optimal

A fragmented approach to liquidity — using separate providers for custody, securities lending, repo, FX and collateral management — creates multiple points of inefficiency:
- **Information gaps**: No single view of available collateral and liquidity across the portfolio
- **Reconciliation friction**: Multiple interfaces and data feeds require significant operational overhead
- **Suboptimal collateral utilisation**: Siloed providers cannot optimise collateral mobility across the full balance sheet

CACEIS's integrated approach solves these problems by providing a single operational ecosystem. As the custodian, CACEIS already holds the full picture of a pension fund's assets. From this position of insight, CACEIS can:
- **Identify lending opportunities** across the entire securities portfolio
- **Manage collateral dynamically** across derivative margining, repo, and lending simultaneously
- **Execute FX hedging** tied directly to underlying currency exposures in the portfolio
- **Deliver repo and securities financing** solutions calibrated to actual portfolio needs

## Securities Finance for Pension Funds

Securities lending is increasingly valuable for pension funds as a yield enhancement tool. CACEIS offers:
- Agency lending and principal lending programmes
- Collateral upgrade/downgrade transactions
- Repo facilities for liquidity management
- Full regulatory compliance (SFTR reporting, indemnification)

## Collateral Management

With growing use of cleared derivatives (EMIR requirements), pension funds need sophisticated collateral management. CACEIS provides:
- Inventory management across all eligible collateral
- Collateral transformation (upgrade/downgrade)
- Tri-party and bilateral collateral management
- Integration with custodian view for instant availability assessment

## FX Integration

Currency risk is a significant concern for pension funds with international allocations. CACEIS's integrated FX offering — covering 70+ currencies, with spot, forwards, swaps and NDFs — enables pension funds to manage FX as part of a unified service rather than engaging a separate FX provider.
