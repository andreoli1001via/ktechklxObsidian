# CACEIS medium term plan 2028: the new strategic horizon

📅 01/26/2026

On 18 November 2025, Crédit Agricole S.A. unveiled its medium-term plan, **ACT 2028**, with the desire to be a conquering bank, leader in Europe, leader in transitions and leader in new technologies. CACEIS is now defining its own strategic priorities to align with these overarching ambitions.

## A Plan Built on Solid Foundations

Leveraging successful initiatives under the 2022–2025 Plan, CACEIS is well positioned to capitalise on current market developments. Key accomplishments include:
- Acquisition of RBC's European asset servicing activities, strengthening position as European leader
- Significant business growth in Luxembourg and Ireland
- New opportunities in Asia with a major operational centre in Malaysia supporting the 'Follow-The-Sun, Data Anytime, Anywhere' model

In line with Crédit Agricole's ACT 2028 Plan, CACEIS establishes its objectives around three major strategic pillars: **Acceleration, Transformation and Cohesion**.

## Acceleration: Expansion and Market Opportunities

CACEIS is extending its European footprint with the opening of a branch in **Stockholm** to serve the Nordic region. In Asia, opening a branch in **Singapore** to support European clientele and build Asian business.

Products and services focus areas:
- **Development of digital assets and fund unit tokenisation**: With its MiCA licence, CACEIS will enable institutional clients to access a secure offering for execution and custody of crypto-assets. Objective: quadruple digital assets under custody within three years.
- **Enhanced ETF and pension fund services**: New solutions for middle-office outsourcing and fund distribution.
- **Market Solutions expansion**: Focus on execution services and collateral optimisation.
- **Growth of alternative assets**: PERES offering rolled out across CACEIS's entire network.
- **New custody and cash services**: Tax-related services, enhancing quality of service.

## Transformation: Efficiency and Commitment

The operational model "Follow the Sun – Data, Anytime, Anywhere", implemented in 2025, will continue its rollout for operational efficiency gains. The Group will extend AI use across all activities. CACEIS's ESG commitments will be key differentiators in the context of European sovereignty.

## Cohesion: Synergies and Cross-Functional Collaboration

CACEIS will construct strategic links with other Crédit Agricole group entities, especially in digital assets. Cross-functional collaboration, internal teamwork and people empowerment will drive innovation, ownership, and client satisfaction.

> **Carlos Rodríguez de Robles**, Deputy CEO in charge of Strategy and Innovation:
> "CACEIS is positioning itself as a consolidator in a shifting European market. Our strength lies in our ability to anticipate change and adapt rapidly to sector transformations. We remain determined to consolidate our position as a European leader and to extend our expertise beyond Europe, with client satisfaction as our sole compass."
