# A world first: Funds and their depositaries enter the tokenised era

📅 04/28/2026

**Montpensier Arbevel Focus Small 2030**, together with CACEIS as depositary, is taking part in the **world's first natively tokenised IPO** on **Lise** (Lightning Stock Exchange).

## What Happened

This marks a historic milestone: for the first time ever, a fund and its depositary (CACEIS) have participated in a natively tokenised IPO on a regulated exchange. "Natively tokenised" means the securities were issued directly in tokenised form on a blockchain — not converted after the fact.

## Lise — Lightning Stock Exchange

Lise is a new European tokenised exchange dedicated to SMEs and mid-cap companies. CACEIS acquired a stake in Kriptown (Lise's parent company) in August 2025 to support the exchange's launch.

Lise operates under MiCA authorisation and enables:
- Native issuance of securities in tokenised form
- 24/7 trading of tokenised securities
- Smart contract-based settlement

## CACEIS's Role

As depositary for Montpensier Arbevel Focus Small 2030, CACEIS:
- Verified the fund's participation in the tokenised IPO
- Safekept the tokenised securities in its crypto-asset custody infrastructure (MiCA-authorised)
- Provided depositary oversight of the tokenisation process

This transaction demonstrates CACEIS's ability to service both traditional funds AND their digital asset investments under a single depositary mandate.

## Context: CACEIS Digital Assets Strategy

This transaction follows a series of milestones in CACEIS's digital assets journey:
1. **June 2025**: CACEIS Bank obtains MiCA authorisation
2. **August 2025**: CACEIS acquires stake in Kriptown/Lise
3. **November 2025**: Amundi tokenises first fund share with CACEIS as tokenisation agent
4. **April 2026**: World's first natively tokenised IPO with CACEIS as depositary
5. **June 2026**: Amundi-Ant International blockchain treasury collaboration with CACEIS as tokenisation agent

CACEIS targets quadrupling digital assets under custody by 2028.
