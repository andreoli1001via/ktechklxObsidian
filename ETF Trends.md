# ETF: Evolving products mean evolving service needs

📅 04/23/2026

Evolution is an important theme and is central to the ongoing growth of ETFs. The first ETFs housed a single share class, passively tracking vanilla equity indexes and this was the status quo for many years. Today the ETF market is far more sophisticated.

## The Evolution of ETF Products

### From Passive to Active
- **Passive ETFs** (index trackers) remain the dominant category
- **Smart beta / factor ETFs** emerged as a second wave
- **Active ETFs** are now the fastest growing segment, increasing from less than 2% of the market to approximately 6-7% depending on the region

### New Structures and Complexities
- **Multi-currency share classes**: The same ETF strategy can be offered in EUR, USD, GBP and other currencies, each requiring separate PCF management and settlement infrastructure
- **Multi-share class ETFs**: Listed and unlisted share classes within a single fund vehicle, enabling distribution through both exchange and traditional TA channels
- **ESG and thematic strategies**: Require enhanced index data inputs and more frequent PCF updates as index compositions change based on ESG screening
- **Fixed income ETFs**: More complex than equity due to OTC bond market dynamics and less liquid underlying instruments

### Active ETFs: The Key Growth Driver
Active ETFs are growing rapidly because they offer:
- Portfolio manager discretion rather than pure index replication
- Lower cost than traditional active mutual funds (due to ETF structure)
- Daily liquidity and transparency
- Access to institutional asset managers who previously only offered mutual fund structures

## Impact on Servicing Requirements

Product evolution is driving service needs evolution:
- **More frequent PCF updates** (Portfolio Composition Files) for active and daily-rebalancing strategies
- **Expanded data integration** with index providers (Bloomberg, MSCI, Ultumus)
- **Multiple settlement models** to support both exchange settlement (via CSDs) and OTC settlement (ICSDs)
- **24/7 monitoring** as ETFs trade across multiple time zones
- **Enhanced reporting** for active strategies and regulatory requirements
