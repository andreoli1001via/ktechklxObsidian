# Governance

## Executive Committee

### Jean Pierre Michalowski — Chief Executive Officer
Prior to being appointed CEO of CACEIS, Jean-Pierre Michalowski was Senior Country Officer of Singapore at Crédit Agricole CIB since 2017. He began his banking career at Meeschaert Rousselle (1988), Banque Indosuez (1989), CIMO (1990), and Credit Lyonnais Rouse (1992). In 1997, he became COO at Crédit Lyonnais Asset Management. In 2003, he joined Crédit Agricole Asset Management. In 2005, he played a role in the creation of CACEIS, serving as CEO of CACEIS Fastnet, CEO of CACEIS Bank (2007) and Deputy CEO of CACEIS Group (2010).

### Jean-Jacques Barbéris — Deputy CEO, Coverage and Digital Assets
Before joining CACEIS, Jean-Jacques Barbéris was head of Institutional and Corporate Clients Coverage and supervised the ESG department at Amundi. He was also chairman of Amundi Germany and CPR asset management since 2021. Previously, he was advisor for Economic and Financial affairs in the staff of the French President (2013–2016) and member of staff of French Minister of Economy Pierre Moscovici. He started his career in the French Treasury Directorate General (2008–2012). Graduate of Ecole Normale Supérieure, Sciences Po Paris and ENA.

### Frédéric Coudreau — Deputy CEO, Business Units and Technology
Frédéric Coudreau began his career at Crédit Lyonnais in 1992, holding project management positions and back offices of market activities. In 2001, he became Head of Organisation and IT for Treasury, FX and Bonds. From 2004–2005 Head of Calyon's Treasury Operations, then back offices and IT for international capital markets. In 2007, Head of Capital Markets Operations. In 2012, Global Head of Operations at Crédit Agricole CIB. Since 2015, Head of Information Systems at Crédit Agricole CIB.

### Philippe Renard — Deputy CEO, Support Functions
Appointed Deputy CEO on October 10th, 2023. Previously CEO of RBC Investor Services Bank in Europe, Luxembourg. Chaired the Investor Services Bank Executive Committee. Over 30 years of experience; prior roles at BNP Benelux. BSc in Chemical Engineering from Liège University.

### Carlos Rodríguez de Robles — Deputy CEO, Strategy and Innovation; MD Spain & LatAm
Appointed Deputy CEO February 11th 2020. Previously CEO of Santander Securities Services (2014). Prior roles at Santander (2004–2014), Banco Santander Mexico (2009), McKinsey (2001–2004), Cemex (1996–1999), Hambros Bank (1995). MBA from Tuck School of Business at Dartmouth College; Bachelor in Economics from Universidad Complutense Madrid.

### Joe Saliba — Deputy General Manager, Origination Countries
Joseph Saliba joined Sabre in 1989, managing operations in France, UK and internationally. In 1999, President of Sabre's management consulting and technology unit. In 2001, joined CGI as President for Europe, Asia Pacific, USA, India, and Business Process Services. Holds a Bachelor of Computer Sciences from Sherbrooke University, Quebec.

### David Bellaiche — Global Head Products & Innovation
Began career at SunGard (1997), joined CLAM/Amundi (2000) as Head of IT development for asset management, then portfolio management. Joined TOBAM in 2006 as quantitative portfolio manager, later Deputy CEO (COO & CFO) for 17 years. Joined Murex in 2023 as Head of Buy-Side Business Line. Joined CACEIS February 2026. Engineering degree from ENAC.

### Bruno Bourbonnaud — Global Head of Market Solutions
Started banking career 1989 at Credit Lyonnais. Head of FI sector within International Trade & Transaction Banking at Crédit Agricole CIB (2015). Joined CACEIS May 2018 as Global Head PERES, then Global Head of Business Development (2021–2023).

### Philippe Bourgues — Global Head PERES Operations
Marine Consulting (1995), Banque CPR (1998), CPR Asset Management (2001–2012), CACEIS Fund Administration (2012), CACEIS Bank France & CACEIS Fund Administration Deputy CEO (2014), Managing Director CACEIS Bank Luxembourg (2017). Appointed Global Head PERES Operations 2026. Engineering degree from ISEP.

### Carine Echelard — Chief Human Resources Officer
Career since 1987 through Caisse de Gestion Mobilière, Nivard Flornoy, Credit Lyonnais, RBC Dexia (2004–2010). Managing Director of CACEIS Bank France and CACEIS Fund Administration (2014). In November 2016 becomes Managing Director in France. Appointed CHRO September 2023.

### Ramy El Houayek — Global Head of Fund Services
Joined CACEIS in 2007. Head of IT Banking and Accounting (2008), Head of IT Fund Administration France (2010), Head of Fund Administration France (2013), Group Head Operational Line Fund Administration (2018), Global Head of Operations Fund Services (2019).

### Jean-Luc Eymery — Chief Compliance Officer
Career from Banque Indosuez (1989 New York), then Paris risk management. COO Indosuez W.I. Carr Securities Hong Kong (1996). London positions at Crédit Agricole CIB (2002–2011). Head of Crédit Agricole CIB strategy team Paris (2011). Graduate from HEC.

### Julien Maze — Chief Financial and Administrative Officer
Joined CACEIS June 2021. Previously Head of Group Financial Control for Crédit Agricole CIB (Paris, 2017), CFO Banque Saudi Fransi (2012–2017), Head of Corporate Planning for Asia at CA CIB HK (2008). Graduate of ESCP Europe and Sciences Po.
