# ATLAS: Advancing CACEIS' Integrated Asset Servicing Model

📅 03/30/2026

Building on its long-standing middle office expertise, CACEIS is enhancing its **Front-to-Back servicing model with ATLAS**.

## ATLAS Overview

ATLAS (Asset Transaction and Lifecycle Administration Services) is CACEIS's integrated front-to-back asset servicing model designed to provide investment managers with a comprehensive, seamless operational solution from trade generation to final custody and accounting.

The ATLAS model allows investment managers to:
- **Consolidate operational complexity** by outsourcing front-to-back operations to a single provider
- **Enhance data visibility** through real-time access to portfolio and operational data
- **Reduce operational risk** by eliminating inter-system interfaces and reconciliation breaks
- **Scale efficiently** as assets under management grow

## Operational Chain

ATLAS covers the full operational lifecycle:

1. **Order Management** — Pre-trade compliance checking, order routing to execution venues
2. **Trade Execution** — Connection to major equity, fixed income, derivatives, and FX execution venues
3. **Trade Capture & Confirmation** — Automated trade booking and counterparty confirmation
4. **Middle Office** — Position keeping, P&L calculation, reconciliation, exception management
5. **Settlement** — Instructions to custodians and CCPs, fail management
6. **Custody** — Safekeeping of assets, corporate actions processing
7. **Fund Accounting** — NAV calculation, expense management
8. **Reporting** — OLIS portal, performance attribution, regulatory reporting

## Technology Foundation

ATLAS is built on CACEIS's significant technology investment:
- **AI Factory**: AI-powered exception detection and process automation
- **OLIS portal**: Real-time client-facing data portal
- Connections to major trading venues and market infrastructure
- Interoperability with client systems (order management systems, risk systems)
