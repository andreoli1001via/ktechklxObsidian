# CACEIS' AI services can help unlock your data's full potential

📅 06/24/2026

*By David Bellaïche, Global Head of Products & Innovation at CACEIS*

In an environment where institutional investors and asset managers are confronted with ever-increasing volumes of data, the important issue is not about collecting more, it's how managers and their investors can use that data for decision-making.

## Investors now have access to vast amounts of data — what is today's main challenge?

With digitalisation, data volumes have skyrocketed: market information, price movements, ESG data, transaction flows, granular portfolio data — often in real time. Yet much of this data remains underutilised, fragmented, difficult to compare and confined to largely backward-looking reports.

The challenge is no longer about collecting ever more data, but about turning what we already have into genuinely actionable intelligence for decision-making. We are entering a third phase: after digitalisation (capturing information) and process optimisation, the question becomes "How do we best harness this data to create value?"

The goal: give investors greater visibility into market trends, a better understanding of risks, and tools to inform asset allocation choices — especially for portfolios combining listed, unlisted and digital assets.

## What specific role can CACEIS play in this new data landscape?

CACEIS sits at the heart of clients' data flows. As a depositary and asset servicer, we process and secure large volumes of information relating to portfolio assets, managed strategies, client flows, ESG data and more. This data underpins the entire range of administrative, control and reporting processes.

Beyond operational efficiency, CACEIS sees a major opportunity: transforming this data into a rich analytical resource. The most strategic information relates to investor flows. By aggregating and anonymising data across the entire client base, CACEIS can surface market trends: asset-class arbitrage, shifts in liquidity, concentration movements. These are direct reflections of what is actually happening in portfolios, mirroring the decisions of international investors.

## How does data translate into value creation for investors?

CACEIS is often the first to detect variations in market liquidity. Using this information, investors can identify potential liquidity shortfalls and position themselves more directly, without relying solely on generic benchmarks. This means faster, better-informed decisions — moving from standardised investment selection to a customised decision-making strategy.

## How is CACEIS's role evolving with AI?

CACEIS is already using AI in a prudent and controlled manner:
- Processing unstructured information (PDF documents, images)
- Converting documents into structured, usable data
- Optimising processes and detecting risks earlier
- **With AI agents that autonomously perform value-added tasks, CACEIS is reducing IT development cycles by around 70%**

AI is prompting a complete rethink of the architecture of processing chains and IT infrastructure.

## How do you reconcile innovation with risk management?

**Governance structure and controls are essential.** With the proliferation of AI tools, a strong governance framework ensures risk mitigation. The winners will not be those with the most tools, but those who integrate these technologies in a controlled, scalable and well-governed manner.

CACEIS takes a balanced approach to cloud and data sovereignty:
- Majority of critical data remains hosted on-premises (full control and compliance)
- Only when strictly necessary is data held in European cloud environments
- No contractual lock-ins with single technology providers
- All data stored in Europe, compliant with EU regulations

**Goal**: enable every client to derive greater value from their own data — greater simplicity, confidence, and potential positive impact on performance.
