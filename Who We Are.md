# Who we are — Your Global Asset Servicing Partner

CACEIS is the asset servicing banking group of Crédit Agricole dedicated to asset managers and institutional investors.

Through offices across Europe, North and South America and Asia, CACEIS offers a broad range of services covering execution, clearing, forex, securities lending, custody, depositary, fund administration, fund distribution support, middle-office outsourcing and issuer services.

CACEIS is a consolidator in the European asset servicing market and posts sustained growth in its business activities. The group holds **€5.9 trillion in assets under custody** and **€3.7 trillion in assets under administration** (figures as of 31 December 2025). Present in **17 countries**.

## Key Areas

### Governance
In addition to the managing bodies (Board of Directors and dedicated committees) and the Crédit Agricole Group structures, CACEIS's governance structure consists of the General Management, the Executive Committee which is made up of the Executive Management.

### Where to Find Us
CACEIS is present in 17 countries and accompanies its clients' development worldwide through its network of sub-custodians in more than 80 countries.

### About Us
With the solid support of reference shareholder Crédit Agricole S.A. CACEIS is a European leader in Asset Servicing and one of the major players worldwide.

### Corporate Social Responsibility
CACEIS takes part in Crédit Agricole Group's Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) initiative through which it has adhered to the ten principles of the UN Global Impact. CACEIS has been awarded a Gold rating from the independent French CSR rating platform EcoVadis.

### Compliance
Compliance ensures the consistency and effectiveness of the prevention and control of non-compliance risks. Its main tasks are to define the applicable normative framework for compliance, contribute to evaluation of non-compliance risks, and represent the Group with regulators, national authorities and professional associations.

### Join Us
CACEIS runs a responsible and innovative social policy focused on employment, career development and the promotion of diversity and equal opportunity. Over 7,000 employees worldwide.
