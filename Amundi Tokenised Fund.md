# Amundi tokenises its first fund in collaboration with CACEIS

📅 11/27/2025

**Amundi**, Europe's leading asset manager, announces the launch of its **first tokenized share** of the **AMUNDI FUNDS CASH EUR** money market fund. The first transaction took place on **November 4th, 2025**, with CACEIS acting as both transfer agent and tokenisation agent.

## Key Details

- **Fund**: AMUNDI FUNDS CASH EUR (money market fund)
- **Tokenised share class**: First tokenised share class of the fund
- **First transaction date**: November 4, 2025
- **CACEIS role**: Transfer agent AND tokenisation agent
- **Technology**: Blockchain-based tokenised shares

## What This Means

This was the first time Amundi had tokenised a share class of one of its funds. "Tokenised" in this context means that units of the fund are represented as digital tokens on a blockchain, enabling:
- Real-time settlement of subscriptions and redemptions
- 24/7 availability (versus traditional T+1 or T+2 settlement)
- Integration with blockchain-based treasury management platforms
- Potential for fractional ownership

## CACEIS's Dual Role

As **transfer agent**, CACEIS maintains the register of fund shareholders and processes subscription/redemption orders.

As **tokenisation agent** (a new role), CACEIS:
- Manages the issuance and redemption of digital tokens representing fund shares
- Maintains the link between the traditional register and the blockchain token ledger
- Ensures the token supply always matches the number of outstanding fund units

## Continuation

This November 2025 transaction was a precursor to the **June 2026 collaboration** between Amundi, CACEIS and Ant International, in which tokenised share classes of the Amundi Money Market Fund – Short Term (EUR and USD) were developed for Ant International's treasury management needs.
